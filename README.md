# Traffic-Intersection-Simulator
# 
# My project is a video game that hopes to achieve a balance between entertainment and education. In my game, the player is looking at a traffic intersection from an # outside perspective and must select the correct order in which the cars currently present will leave the intersection with various obstacles providing more depth # # to the problem depending on the difficulty they have chosen on the screen prior.
#
# The reason why I decided to make a game loosely based on scenarios that may arise in the futures of drivers is because I hope to earn my driving license by the end # of the year, and the research conducted will most certainly help me in that regard as well.
